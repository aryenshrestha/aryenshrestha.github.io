Hello! I am a first-year PhD student in Public Policy (Economics Track) at the Harvard Kennedy School. I am interested in problems of local governance, especially as they relate to **development, public finance, political economy, and housing**. You can find my research projects and policy writing [here]({{< relref "research" >}}).

Previously, I was an Economics Research Specialist at Dartmouth College, working with Professors Elizabeth Cascio and Ethan Lewis. I examined the impact of major policy changes in 20th-century US history, including immigration reform and school desegregation. I received my BA in Economics from Amherst College in 2024. I was born and raised in Kathmandu, Nepal.

Outside of academia, I enjoy reading, watching movies, taking photos, and spending time in nature.
